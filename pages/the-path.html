Chapter 04 — The Path

Two roads to working freely,
running in parallel.

You have not one but two independent paths to the same destination. They run side by side. You can travel both at once. No path closes off another.

Path A — The EAD route.

This is the path tied to my green-card progress. You arrive on H-4. We wait for my I-140 to approve. You apply for the H-4 EAD. Once it lands, you can work, freelance, monetize, hire, sell, anything.

Path B — The student route.

This is the path entirely independent of my timeline. You arrive on H-4. You apply to a program for the next intake. You establish state residency for in-state tuition. You enroll. In your final semester, you switch to F-1. Graduation hands you OPT — twelve months, or thirty-six if STEM. From there, an employer can sponsor your own H-1B.

The point isn't to pick the right path. The point is that we have two of them, and they reinforce each other if we walk them together.
— Why this matters

A possible first year, just to make it concrete.

Here's what twelve months might look like, broken into quarters. Adjust the channel cadence, school cadence, and travel cadence to taste.

Settling in.

Arrive on H-4. SSN or ITIN. Driver's license. Bank accounts. First videos shot in your new kitchen. Scout colleges and programs. Discover your new neighborhood. Find a grocery store that stocks the right kind of rice.

Building.

Apply to a program for the next intake. Channel: consistent upload schedule. Establish state residency formally. Weekends spent exploring the country, short trips, long drives, the kind of America that doesn't make it onto Instagram.

Studying and creating.

Coursework begins if admitted. Channel growing, monetization still off. Network with other Indian creators in the US. The first time someone you don't know recognizes you at a desi grocery store.

Unlocking.

I-140 likely approved around here. Apply for H-4 EAD immediately. Channel monetization flips on. The two years of audience-building start paying. Future you, unobstructed.

None of this is settled. All of it is workable. The point isn't that I have planned your life, it's that I have thought about it carefully, and the answers I have found make me more confident, not less, that this is a good move for both of us.
