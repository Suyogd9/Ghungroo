My situation, so the rest makes sense

Right now
H-1B (work visa). This is my legal status in the US. Your H-4 will be linked to this — you're a "dependent" on my status.
Started Feb 2026
I-140: PWD step. PWD = Prevailing Wage Determination. It's step 1 of the green card process. Order: PWD → PERM → I-140 filing → I-140 approval. Usually takes 1–2 years total.
After I-140
You unlock H-4 EAD. Once my I-140 is approved, you become eligible for an H-4 EAD (work permit). Until then, H-4 has work restrictions — but plenty of options. Details below.

What is an H-4 visa, actually?

It's a "dependent" visa. It lets the spouse of someone on H-1B live legally in the US for the duration of the H-1B holder's stay.

You CAN

  • Live in the US legally
  • Travel in & out of the country
  • Open a bank account, get an SSN/ITIN
  • Get a driver's license
  • Study full-time or part-time
  • Volunteer (unpaid work)

You CANNOT (yet)

  • Work for pay in the US
  • Receive paid YouTube revenue while on plain H-4
  • Run a US-registered business as employment
  • Be self-employed for income

The unlock

  • After my I-140 is approved, you apply for H-4 EAD
  • EAD = Employment Authorization Document
  • Then: you can work, freelance, monetize, anything

Not legal advice. This is my best understanding from research and from my own filings — we'll confirm anything specific with my company's immigration lawyer before any decision or filing.
