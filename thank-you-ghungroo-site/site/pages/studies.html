Studying on H-4 — your secret weapon

Three reasons this is worth considering, even if you weren't planning to study.

1. You can study
Full stop. H-4 lets you enroll in full-time or part-time programs at any accredited US college or university. No special permission needed.
2. In-state tuition
Because we'll be physically residing in a state (in our case, [state]), you're treated as a resident for tuition purposes after meeting the residency requirement (usually 12 months). In-state tuition is often 2–4× cheaper than out-of-state.
3. F-1 → OPT path
In your final semester you can switch from H-4 to F-1 (student visa). After graduating, F-1 gives you OPT (Optional Practical Training) — up to 12 months of legal work in your field, or 36 months if you pick a STEM-designated program. Even without EAD, you'd have a clean path to a US job.
