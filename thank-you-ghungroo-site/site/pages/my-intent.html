02 · My Intent

As your future husband, here's what I'm signing up for

Tujhe "khup samjudar ahese" mhntalis na — these are the things behind that word.

01

Tujhe aai-baba majhe pan aai-baba

"It's the last thing on earth me tyana hurt hotana baghen" — he tu Mother's Day la bollelis. Tujhi he goshta majhi pan ahe ata. Pune trips frequent, video calls roj, ani jevha pan garaj asel — me ani tu doghe tikde asu. [Family situation] barobar pan apan halu halu kaam karu.

02

Your dreams aren't "after marriage" items

Arangetram, the channel, your own business idea — these don't get paused after marriage. H-4 cha vel = building runway. EAD nantar = takeoff. Mich tujha first subscriber, first sponsor lead, first investor (ego aside) honare.

03

Partnership, not permission

Tu konashi bolaychas, kuthe jayachas, kay kharedi karaychas, kay shikaychas — yat me ek vote ahe, veto nahi. Tujha 10-to-7 work-life balance, tujhe friend circles, tujha alcohol collection 😂 — he tujha space ahe, ani te tasach rahnar.

04

Direct, no games, no guessing

Tu mhntalis "tase sweet nahi" mulanbaddal — me promise karto, the over-performing politeness is just for the first month. Khare mhnje, me "baat toh sahi hei" type ahe. Naraj jhalis tar sang, anandi jhalis tar sang, doubt asla tar sang. Me overthink karnar nhi tujhe mood swings. (You can, though. "All days are not same" — lakshyat ahe.)

05

The boring adult stuff = handled

H-4 paperwork, taxes, insurance, navin gharachi setup, US madhli initial 6 months chi adjustment — yacha tension tula nahi gheaycha. Me ahe na. Tu fakta apla suti suti suchnari poetry, dance practice, recipes, ani parents la calls — he kar.

None of this is a contract.
It's a starting list.
We'll add more together. ❤️
